Geometric starters: exact o is an analytic circle, simple a adds a right stem, and two-storey a adds an analytic upper arch. The b/d ascenders and p/q descenders reuse the same circle with left or right stems. The n/u/h/m forms use analytic quadratic humps or bowls. The c/e open loop samples a circular arc; i uses a vertical stem and separate dot, while í replaces its dot with an acute; j has a dot and quadratic descender. The g reuses the o bowl plus a curved right descender. The l/t use exact straight stems and t also shares the e-style crossbar; f has a taller stem. The r uses a quadratic shoulder, and s uses four joined quadratic arcs. The v/w, k, x, y and z forms use exact straight ink. Acute ó/á add an accent. Native v1 TSV/PDF ink samples these curves as line segments rather than preserving exact circle or quadratic primitives. The explicit replacement affects only the selected grapheme.

Author a glyph to preview a rotation.

Rotation changes every selected glyph path around an explicit pivot. Positive angles turn clockwise on the physical page. Rounded pen caps must remain in the cell; a collapsed segment or clipped contour refuses atomically. The preview never modifies the authored TSV.

Author a glyph to preview its position.

Move every path of the selected glyph together to adjust its position without deforming ink. Positive X moves right; positive Y moves down. Every round pen cap must remain inside the cell. Preview is read-only; apply and download are separate deliberate actions.

Author a glyph to preview its adjusted pen thickness.

Ink adjustment changes only the pen thickness of the selected exact Unicode glyph; positions, directions, stroke order and colors remain unchanged. The preview does not edit your draft. A width outside 80–1500 µm or any clipped round pen cap refuses atomically. Download the TSV explicitly after applying the adjustment.

Undo and redo recover only the latest accepted whole-glyph slant, height, width, move, rotation or ink adjustment. Exact TSV snapshots keep rounding loss reversible until a later edit, import or session reset. This is one step, not persistent revision history.

Refused angles, clipping or missing glyph ink leave the current unsaved draft untouched. Applying twice compounds the tilt. One undo/redo step tracks the latest accepted glyph transform; other ink edits, a TSV load or a session reset clear that step. Save an original TSV before transforming.

A path shift moves all its directed segments together in micrometres. Enter a signed offset for either axis. A zero shift or any clipped pen contour is refused without changing the unsaved ink.

Close adds a straight segment from an open continuous path's last endpoint to its first. Both ends must be within 1200 µm and the path must have two strokes. The authored contour is preserved; it is not an analytic curve.

For a closed continuous loop, select a stroke after its current first stroke, then choose Start closed pen loop to change where the pen begins without moving ink. Open paths and single strokes cannot use this action.

Path reversal flips the order and direction of every touching segment in the selected path. Unrelated strokes and Unicode glyphs keep their positions; a path needs at least two segments. Path movement swaps the whole selected path with the previous or next path.

Connect adds an explicit short straight stroke to the next segment of the same glyph. It requires the same pen width/color and a gap at most 1200 µm; no automatic cursive or inferred pen lift is created.
