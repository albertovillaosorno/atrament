Notebook page profile

Notebook date and printed-page preferences. PDF/PNG draws the date line or box in the top margin. An optional numeric date prints inside it (not generated handwriting), requiring 10 mm top clearance and 36 mm usable width. Printed page numbers need 7 mm bottom clearance, and PDF numbers pages 1–32 (PNG can select one of those pages). A nonzero border radius draws a rounded printable outline; handwriting is not yet drawn. Preprinted sheets omit the existing ruling but retain selected annotations. Use current paper to include the sheet editor settings.

Authored glyph strokes

Draw exact Unicode glyph templates as physical line segments. Edit coordinates with ranges and see the shape below. No automatic font or handwriting synthesis. The downloaded TSV is validated by the Rust CLI on import.

Local draft only

Geometric starters: exact o is an analytic circle, simple a adds a right stem, and two-storey a adds an analytic upper arch. The b/d ascenders and p/q descenders reuse the same circle with left or right stems. The n/u/h/m forms use analytic quadratic humps or bowls. The c/e open loop samples a circular arc; i uses a vertical stem and separate dot, while í replaces its dot with an acute; j has a dot and quadratic descender. The g reuses the o bowl plus a curved right descender. The l/t use exact straight stems and t also shares the e-style crossbar; f has a taller stem. The r uses a quadratic shoulder, and s uses four joined quadratic arcs. The v/w, k, x, y and z forms use exact straight ink. Acute ó/á add an accent. Native v1 TSV/PDF ink samples these curves as line segments rather than preserving exact circle or quadratic primitives. The explicit replacement affects only the selected grapheme.

The acute forms use an explicit rising accent stroke above the highest story, positioned from geometry and pen width. A two-storey á may require reducing the radius. Shift or lift with the controls. If any ink cannot fit, no glyph is replaced.

Fill missing lowercase adds only letters without authored strokes using the currently selected center, radius, ink color and pen width. Existing letters remain as drawn. Review and download the TSV explicitly; this does not add uppercase, accents, or automatic handwritten text. Fill missing Spanish accents separately copies authored lowercase bases into á, é, í, ó, ú, ñ, ü (NFC). The decomposed button independently constructs their seven NFD forms, keeping exact Unicode keys and custom target ink separate. A missing base, ambiguous dotted i or clipped mark refuses the entire batch. Basic punctuation can be filled independently: . , ! ¡ ? ¿, including exact inverse exclamation and sampled question curves. A separate quotation fill adds only missing « » ‘ ’ “ ” shapes. The angle brackets are exact chevrons; the upper curly quotes use sampled quadratic strokes. These forms retain independent Unicode identities and earlier authored ink. Fill missing basic math separately adds +, ASCII -, Unicode minus −, =, × and ÷ from exact straight pen paths. Division dots must remain separated by a visible gap. All forms keep independent Unicode keys and existing author ink. Numeral 0 is an exact SVG ellipse (distinct from circular o), 8 uses two ellipses, and 1–7/9 combine ordered lines, arcs or loops. The separate digit fill preserves authored shapes and only updates the local unsaved draft. The uppercase A–Z fill similarly preserves authored capitals. O and Q reuse the exact o circle guide, C and G use a circular open arc, and S and other curved capitals use analytic quadratics. Only exact straight strokes and sampled curve chords export. Every fill remains unsaved.
